Title: ZenoTech Acquires NeuroBotics.ai to Expand Healthcare AI Portfolio
Date: January 28, 2025

ZenoTech Inc. has announced the acquisition of NeuroBotics.ai, a leading startup specializing in neurological diagnostic tools powered by AI. This move strengthens ZenoTech’s presence in the digital health domain and is aligned with its strategic roadmap for 2025.

The acquisition deal, valued at $42 million, will integrate NeuroBotics’s flagship product — BrainCheckX — into ZenoTech's HealthVision suite.

Title: ZenoTech Launches ZenoCloud 3.0 with Built-in AI Ops
Date: April 11, 2025

ZenoTech today unveiled ZenoCloud 3.0, the latest version of its enterprise cloud platform, featuring built-in AI Ops and adaptive scaling for enterprise workloads. ZenoCloud 3.0 reduces cloud costs by up to 20% through intelligent workload forecasting.

CEO Angela Roberts said, “ZenoCloud 3.0 is our biggest leap forward in autonomous cloud management.”
