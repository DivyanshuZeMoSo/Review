Title: Why Predictive Analytics is Changing the Game for Businesses
Author: Sarah Lin, ZenoTech Insights Team
Date: March 4, 2025

Predictive analytics, powered by machine learning models, allows businesses to forecast trends and customer behavior with remarkable accuracy. At ZenoTech, our clients in retail and logistics have reported up to a 27% improvement in inventory optimization using our platforms.

We’re working on embedding explainable AI (XAI) into our analytics dashboards. This helps decision-makers trust and interpret model predictions, fostering a data-driven culture within enterprises.
