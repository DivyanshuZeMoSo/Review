=== Financial Report: ZenoTech Inc. (2024) ===

Company: ZenoTech Inc.
Fiscal Year: 2024

Revenue: $145 million (increase of 18% YoY)
Gross Profit Margin: 62%
Operating Income: $27 million
Net Profit: $18.5 million
Earnings per Share (EPS): $2.15

Highlights:
- Strong performance in the AI SaaS division, contributing $52 million in revenue.
- International expansion accounted for 30% of total revenue.
- R&D investment increased by 22% to $11 million.
- Debt-to-equity ratio improved to 0.48 from 0.65.

Future Outlook:
- Projected revenue growth for FY 2025: 15–18%
- Focus on cloud automation, predictive analytics, and healthcare AI platforms.

=== Press Release: ZenoTech Acquires NeuroBotics.ai ===
Date: January 28, 2025

ZenoTech Inc. has announced the acquisition of NeuroBotics.ai, a leading startup specializing in neurological diagnostic tools powered by AI. This move strengthens ZenoTech’s presence in the digital health domain and is aligned with its strategic roadmap for 2025.

The acquisition deal, valued at $42 million, will integrate NeuroBotics’s flagship product — BrainCheckX — into ZenoTech's HealthVision suite.
